Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how Stackly Studio handles information when you visit this website or contact the studio.

Information you choose to share

The inquiry form opens a draft in your email application; it does not submit information to this website. If you send that email, or contact us directly, we receive the details in your message and the sender information provided by your email service. We use it to respond and manage the conversation.

Website services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Your browser requests those assets from their providers, who may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Their handling of that information is described in their own privacy policies.

Storage and sharing

This site does not include an account system or a server-side inquiry database. Messages sent by email are handled through the email services used by you and the studio. We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when needed to operate communications or where required by law.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to send an inquiry. For questions about information you have shared with the studio, contact hello@stackly.studio.
